<h1 align="center">Muses · Erato</h1>
<p align="center">Your collection, on the move.</p>
<p align="center"><a href="https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses/releases/tag/erato%2Fv1.0.0-beta.2">Explore the iOS beta</a> · <a href="https://xiaotwu.github.io/Project-Muses/ios/">Product & support</a></p>

**Erato** brings the [Muses family](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses) to iPhone and iPad. Inspired by Polyhymnia on the Mac, it combines discovery, a personal collection and a persistent player in an interface that adapts to the device in your hands.

## Keep your collection close

- Search and browse videos, channels and playlists.
- Save favorites, organize editable playlists and revisit your viewing history.
- Choose Music or Video layouts while keeping playback in place.
- Manage your queue, select an entry, reorder items and clear Up Next.
- Write notes and save time bookmarks alongside a video.
- Enjoy adaptive iPhone and iPad navigation, cards and lists.

## Designed for everyday use

A content-first Home, clear library categories and compact player controls keep your collection easy to explore. Notes and bookmarks help you return to the moments you want to remember.

## Availability

Requires **iOS 18 or later**. Erato is currently in beta, with separate Public and experimental Native editions. The Public edition uses visible video playback and pauses when the player closes or the app enters the background. Experimental background audio in the Native edition still requires further validation.

Beta installation depends on the edition's signing and device requirements. See the [release notes](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses/releases/tag/erato%2Fv1.0.0-beta.2) and [installation guide](https://xiaotwu.github.io/Project-Muses/installation.html) before choosing a version.
