Using Muses-Erato

Muses-Erato is an iOS app for finding YouTube videos and organizing a local library, playlists, notes and time bookmarks. These terms describe use of Muses-Erato. Review the privacy policy to understand how the app and its service providers handle data.

YouTube services

Muses-Erato uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Muses-Erato is an independent app and is not an official Google or YouTube product.

Content availability and playback depend on YouTube, the content owner, your region, your account and network access. Some videos cannot play in embedded players. YouTube may display advertisements. Muses-Erato does not grant rights to content, remove provider restrictions, or provide background audio or offline video downloads.

Your library and Google access

Local library changes do not modify your YouTube playlists or subscriptions. Optional Google sign-in requests read-only access for supported account views. You can sign out in the app or review access in Google Account permissions.

Use the app's local-data controls to manage saved information. Deleting an editable item does not automatically erase an immutable recovery copy. Full local-data deletion includes app-managed recovery copies; pending cleanup must finish before deletion is complete. These actions do not delete your Google account or YouTube content. See the privacy policy for the scope and limits of these controls.
