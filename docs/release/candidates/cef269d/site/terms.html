Unpublished candidate cef269d. These terms require final owner/provider review before publication.

The candidate includes an undocumented YouTube Music Home interface separate from the official YouTube Data API. Availability and account-personalization cannot be guaranteed. Local playlists and library edits do not modify the YouTube account. Local sign-out removes app credentials and authorized caches; Revoke access also attempts Google permission revocation. Required deletion, retention and provider privacy decisions remain unresolved.
