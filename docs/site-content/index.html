Muses-Erato

A personal library and notebook for YouTube videos on iOS.

Developed by xiaotwu, Muses-Erato brings video search, a local library and a visible YouTube player together in one app.

Keep your collection close

  • Search YouTube videos, channels and playlists, or open a supported YouTube link.
  • Save video selections, mark favorites and organize local playlists.
  • Manage your playback queue and revisit local viewing history.
  • Write notes and save time bookmarks alongside a video.

Optional Google sign-in

Connect Google to view supported YouTube channel, playlist and subscription information with read-only access. Local favorites and playlists do not change your YouTube account. Google sign-in is optional.

Playback and your data

Videos play in the visible YouTube player, which may show advertisements. Playback pauses when the player screen closes or the app enters the background. Muses-Erato does not offer background audio or offline video downloads. Videos designated Made for Kids or whose audience/embedding status cannot be verified are not loaded in the app player; you can choose to open them in YouTube.

Google receives information through search, thumbnails, authentication and playback. Read the privacy policy for local storage, recovery copies and deletion controls, and the terms of use for service conditions.

Get help

Contact support through GitHub Issues. Issues are public. Describe the behavior and app version without posting account details, passwords, tokens, verification codes or private notes.
